The Fall of the Five

"Every villain was once a hero. Every corrupted soul was once pure."

The Lore of Moogusverse

Long before the present age, the world known as Moogusverse was on the verge of destruction. Endless wars, disasters, and monsters threatened every civilization. To preserve balance, the Heart of Creation forged five divine guardians, each representing one of the purest virtues ever known.

They were not rulers. They were protectors.

Together, they were called The Five Virtues:

  • Humility
  • Truth
  • Justice
  • Modesty
  • Diligence

Each guardian was entrusted with protecting humanity and guiding the people toward peace. For centuries, they fulfilled their purpose perfectly... Until humanity betrayed them.

The Corruption

As Moogusverse entered an age of prosperity, the people slowly abandoned the very virtues that had saved them.

  • Kings became greedy.
  • Merchants lied.
  • Judges accepted corruption.
  • The powerful mocked the weak.
  • The lazy expected everything without effort.

The Five Guardians continued protecting them despite witnessing these changes. But every betrayal, every lie, every injustice, and every act of arrogance stained the hearts of their creators.

Their divine souls slowly cracked. Virtues slowly transformed into sins. One by one... they fell.

1. King of Pride

Former Title: The King of Humility

Before Corruption

Originally, the King of Humility believed no ruler should ever stand above the people. He walked among ordinary citizens without revealing his identity, listened to every complaint, and always placed others before himself.

His greatest strength came from humility. The more selfless he became, the stronger his light shined.

People once called him... "The King Who Never Sat Upon His Throne."

Corruption

Eventually, the people began seeing his kindness as weakness. They ignored his warnings, mocked his mercy, and questioned his authority. Even those he had personally saved betrayed him.

After centuries of endless disappointment, he reached one conclusion:

"Perhaps kindness deserves no reward."

Humility shattered. Pride was born. He crowned himself, declaring that only perfection deserved to rule. Now he believes everyone exists beneath him.

Personality & Relations

Toward Master of Deceit: "Useful... but never trustworthy."

Toward Guardian of Silence: "You held onto hope longer than anyone. Foolish."

Toward Sun of Tropicals: "Your arrogance finally caught up to mine."

Toward Queen of Sloth: "Even asleep... you remain surprisingly wise."

2. Master of Deceit

Former Title: Master of Truth

Before Corruption

Truth believed every problem could be solved through honesty. He exposed criminals, protected innocent people, and never once spoke a lie. His words were absolute. If Truth declared something... it became undeniable.

Corruption

People feared truth. They preferred comfortable lies. Governments erased history, citizens spread false accusations, and even innocent people rejected the truth if it hurt their pride.

Truth realized something terrifying:

"People don't want truth. They want lies that make them happy."

His heart collapsed. Truth became Deceit. Now every sentence he speaks contains hidden meanings. Nothing he says can ever be trusted.

Personality & Relations

Toward King of Pride: "A magnificent king... who mistakes ego for greatness."

Toward Guardian of Silence: "Still believing justice exists? How adorable."

Toward Sun of Tropicals: "Your smile hides anger better than mine."

Toward Queen of Sloth: "The only one patient enough to enjoy my games."

3. Guardian of Silence

Former Title: Guardian of Justice

Before Corruption

Justice never chose sides. Every soul received equal judgment. Whether king or beggar... everyone stood equal before him. He carried the legendary Soul of Justice, a divine essence said to be incapable of corruption.

Because of this, he remained pure far longer than the other four. While Humility became Pride, Truth became Deceit, Modesty became Arrogance, and Diligence weakened... Justice still fought. He refused to abandon humanity.

He believed: "As long as the Soul of Justice still shines... the world can still be saved."

For centuries... he fought alone.

The Final Fall

One day... the Soul of Justice chose humanity over its own guardian. The people ignored justice, protected criminals, punished innocents, and celebrated corruption. Justice watched guilty kings receive praise while innocent children suffered.

The Soul of Justice finally cracked. Not because Justice gave up, but because humanity no longer wanted justice.

His final words before corruption were: "If justice has no place in this world... then silence shall judge instead."

Justice vanished. Guardian of Silence was born. Now... he believes the fairest judgment is the complete absence of judgment. If nobody can speak... nobody can lie. If nobody can move... nobody can commit injustice.

Personality & Relations

Toward King of Pride: "Power without fairness creates only tyranny."

Toward Master of Deceit: "You abandoned truth before anyone else."

Toward Sun of Tropicals: "Your warmth became wildfire."

Toward Queen of Sloth: "Sleep... is kinder than the world we failed to protect."

Although corrupted... deep inside him remains a tiny fragment of the Soul of Justice. It occasionally causes him to hesitate before punishing innocent people. The other four believe that one day... Justice may awaken again.

4. Sun of Tropicals

Former Title: Sun of Modesty

Before Corruption

He never sought praise. Every flower that bloomed, every forest restored, every life saved... he quietly walked away afterward. Nature itself loved his humble heart.

Corruption

People exploited nature, burned forests, destroyed oceans, and then blamed nature for fighting back. When they finally begged for help... they praised themselves instead of their savior.

His humility disappeared. Now he believes only he deserves to be admired. His forests exist to glorify him. His sunlight burns those who refuse to acknowledge his greatness.

Personality & Relations

Toward King of Pride: "One throne isn't large enough for both of us."

Toward Master of Deceit: "You're entertaining... until you lie to me."

Toward Guardian of Silence: "You're far too serious."

Toward Queen of Sloth: "Wake up once in a while."

5. Queen of Sloth

Former Title: Queen of Diligence

Before Corruption

She never rested. Day and night she rebuilt villages, healed the sick, guided children, and worked harder than anyone. She believed effort could overcome every obstacle.

Corruption

People stopped trying. They demanded miracles and expected others to solve every problem. When helped... they became even lazier.

Eventually... she realized she was working harder than the people she protected. Her spirit broke.

"If they refuse to move... then why should I?"

Diligence became Sloth. She now rules over dreams, endless rest, and peaceful surrender.

Personality & Relations

Toward King of Pride: "He's loud... but dependable."

Toward Master of Deceit: "His lies are exhausting."

Toward Guardian of Silence: "He still carries guilt."

Toward Sun of Tropicals: "Too energetic."

Current Relationship

Despite becoming corrupted, the five still see each other as family. They rarely admit it, but the bond they formed while protecting Moogusverse has never completely disappeared.

  • King of Pride naturally assumes leadership, and the others usually follow—not out of fear, but because they remember the dependable guardian he once was.
  • Master of Deceit constantly teases and manipulates the others, yet secretly gathers information to protect the group from outside threats.
  • Guardian of Silence often disagrees with the others' cruelty. Though corrupted, he still hesitates when innocent lives are involved because a faint fragment of the Soul of Justice remains within him.
  • Sun of Tropicals is the emotional heart of the group, quick to anger and quick to laugh. He frequently clashes with King of Pride over who deserves more recognition.
  • Queen of Sloth acts as the mediator. She rarely speaks, but when she does, the other four usually listen. Beneath her sleepy demeanor lies the same compassionate guardian who once worked tirelessly to save the world.

Deep within their corrupted souls, the memories of the Five Virtues still linger. They have not forgotten the promise they made to protect Moogusverse—they simply no longer believe its people deserve to be saved. That lingering conflict leaves open the possibility that, under the right circumstances, fragments of their original selves could one day resurface.
